# Airbnb.com.au - Recommendation and Outcome Tracking

Use this file to turn host intelligence into an improving decision system. The
system should not only say what to change; it should record whether the change
worked.

## Decision rules

- Every recommendation states the target metric, expected effect, confidence,
  and downside risk.
- Every accepted recommendation becomes an action log entry.
- Every action has a pre-window, post-window, and confounder notes.
- Do not claim causal lift when demand context, market shocks, or simultaneous
  changes make attribution weak.

## `airbnb_recommendation`

Candidate action generated by the system.

Fields:

- `recommendation_id`
- `listing_id`
- `recommendation_type`
- `target_dates`
- `target_guest_segment`
- `problem_statement`
- `evidence_refs`
- `expected_metric`
- `expected_direction`
- `expected_value_range`
- `confidence`
- `risk_level`
- `recommended_action`
- `do_not_do_reason`
- `created_at`
- `status`

Examples:

- raise peak date price by 12-18%
- reduce orphan-night minimum stay to 1 night
- disable Smart Pricing for a rule-set period
- replace hero photo with view or bedroom photo
- add missing parking or self-check-in amenity
- send manual check-in clarification after skipped scheduled message

## Recommendation types

| Type | Evidence to require |
|---|---|
| Price increase | Booking pace ahead of target, comp price index low, demand context strong, quality not weak |
| Price decrease | Booking pace behind target, comp price index high, conversion weak, margin still safe |
| Restriction change | Search dates unbookable due to min stay/check-in/out rules and enough margin exists |
| Promotion | Eligible dates, weak pace, margin safety, no peak-demand leakage |
| Content change | Search-card or listing conversion weak, photo/title/amenity gap visible |
| Operations intervention | Message skip, review theme recurrence, turnover overload, maintenance recurrence |
| Compliance review | Airbnb-visible field mismatch, regulation signal, missing registration/permit field |

## Outcome review cadence

| Action | Review window |
|---|---:|
| Price change | 7, 14, and 30 days, or after booking |
| Minimum-stay/rule change | 7 and 21 days |
| Promotion | End of promotion plus 14 days |
| Hero photo/title/content change | 14 and 30 days |
| Message workflow change | Next 5 eligible reservations |
| Cleaning/maintenance intervention | Next 5 stays and next review cycle |

## Decision dashboard

Track:

- recommendations created
- recommendations accepted
- recommendations rejected with reason
- actions implemented
- actions rolled back
- measured wins/losses/inconclusive
- average margin impact
- repeated failed recommendation patterns
- stale recommendations with expired evidence
