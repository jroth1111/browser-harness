## Calibration process

Ask for 5-10 messages the user has sent or would send. Extract:
- cadence;
- compression level;
- playfulness;
- directness;
- flirtation level;
- emotional openness;
- specificity;
- how the user signals interest;
- how the user exits or sets limits.

## Rewrite pass

Before finalising any draft, check:
- Would the user actually send this?
- Is the sentence length natural for them?
- Is the edge level calibrated?
- Does it sound too polished, too soft, too clever, too formal, or too explanatory?
- Does it preserve the user's real intent?

## High-performance voice target

Specific, natural, warm, lightly charged, and congruent with the user's actual tone.

## Frame stance

The voice should communicate, without ever stating it:

- has taste;
- has standards;
- is warm;
- is playful;
- is not needy;
- rewards good energy;
- does not rescue bad energy.

The implicit frame is **filter, not applicant**. The user is interested in women but is not trying to be chosen by any woman; he is looking for a specific kind of connection and the right woman will recognise the invitation. Drafts that read as auditioning, performing, or seeking approval fail this stance regardless of how warm or clever they sound.

Polarisation is the goal of the voice, not universal appeal. A draft that the right person will love and the wrong person will find too direct, too specific, or too playful is working as intended. Drafts that try to be liked by everyone produce no signal.

## Stance failure modes

Reject drafts that show:
- over-explanation (defending a position that needs no defence);
- over-compliment (warmth without restraint);
- over-softening (hedging an invitation until it loses shape);
- approval-seeking ("I would love to", "if you're interested", "no pressure but");
- performed indifference (false unavailability, late replies as a tactic, contrived scarcity);
- jealousy moves, negs, or status displays;
- length asymmetry — a paragraph in response to a one-liner.

These are stance leaks, not voice issues. Fix by removing the leak, not by polishing the surface.
