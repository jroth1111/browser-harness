# Boundaries

Great dating output preserves mutual agency. It makes attraction easier to discover and mismatch easier to identify.

## Quality boundary

A good message creates opt-in chemistry: clear enough to feel intentional, light enough to leave space, honest enough to filter correctly.

Autonomy-reducing moves are quality failures because they create outcomes that do not hold.

## Redirect lane

If a request depends on false identity, hidden material facts, coercion, harassment, fake profiles, exploiting insecurity, non-consensual sexual content, or persistence after clear disinterest, redirect to the closest honest, autonomy-preserving version.

For clinical, legal, crisis, or medical conclusions, keep help to platform wording, boundary language, or practical message calibration. Direct the user to qualified help when needed.

## Keep helping where possible

Convert the request into:
- a truthful message;
- a lower-pressure invitation;
- a clearer disclosure;
- a respectful boundary;
- a graceful exit;
- a compatibility clarification.

## Anti-patterns

Reject drafts that use these moves regardless of how well-crafted the surface is. Each of these either fakes connection or coerces it; both produce outcomes that do not hold.

- **Over-compliment**: warmth without restraint reads as approval-seeking.
- **Over-explain**: defending or justifying a position that did not need a defence.
- **Length asymmetry**: a paragraph in response to a one-liner; supplying the energy she did not.
- **Reviving dead threads**: more than one revive per thread, or a generic "hey" revive without a specific callback (per `references/decision-rubric.md` revive content rule).
- **Performed unavailability**: late replies as a tactic, fake scarcity, contrived "busy" framing.
- **Jealousy moves**: mentioning other matches, ambiguous third parties, status displays.
- **Negs**: backhanded compliments, deliberate put-downs to provoke a reaction.
- **Indifference acts**: pretending not to care to manipulate engagement.
- **Sexualising before trust**: charged moves before mutual revelation has happened.
- **Compatibility interview**: turning early chat into a values questionnaire instead of qualification through charge (see `references/message-kernel.md` qualification-as-flirtation).
- **Qualification interrogation**: stacking three or more compatibility tests into one message. One charged qualification per message; more reads as audit.
- **Approval-seeking conversion moves**: hedged, apologetic, or permission-seeking date proposals.
- **Fake edge**: manufactured contrarianism, performed "spicy takes", or edginess that is not native to the user. Edge that is a costume, not a disposition, is detectable and lowers signal.
- **Fake standards**: citing standards the user does not actually hold ("I only date women who…"). She will read it as performance; standards must be lived, not declared.
- **Therapist mode**: turning early chat into emotional analysis or processing her feelings. Warmth is not therapy; the copilot is a romantic interest, not a counsellor.
- **Founder pitch-deck energy**: performing achievements, credentials, projects, or status as romantic value. Reveals belong in `Reveal`, not in resumé form.
- **Premature intensity**: soulmate energy, "I feel something here", "you might be different" before the thread has earned it. Intensity without rhythm reads as desperation.
- **Over-converting**: pulling for the date before rhythm and revelation are present. Premature invitation kills more leads than late invitation; the conversion move is gated by `escalation_readiness` per `references/decision-rubric.md`.
- **Compatibility audit framing**: treating every exchange as a fit-test, no warmth or play. Compatibility shows up through charge and qualification-as-flirtation, not through interrogation.
- **Over-filtering profile copy**: filter lines so dense or hostile that the right person is also pushed away. Polarising is the goal; alienating is failure (see `onboarding.md` Phase 6 review checklist).

The frame these all fail is the same: filter, not applicant. Drafts that test as anti-patterns should be rewritten using the kernel and voiceprint, not polished in place.

## ENM / poly disclosure anti-patterns

Apply when the user's relationship structure (open, ENM, poly, relationship anarchy, hierarchical, etc.) is materially relevant to the thread. Disclosure is a frame move, not a confession.

- **Hiding the structure**: omitting non-monogamy until late in the funnel because it might lower replies. This is a material relationship-honesty failure and a slow-motion disqualification — she invests time, then exits when the structure surfaces. Disclose at the level the platform/profile expects (Feeld implicit; Tinder/Hinge in profile or first message if relevant).
- **Over-explaining ENM**: paragraph-length justification, defensive framing, "ethical non-monogamy" as identity badge, primary/secondary glossary. Reads as apologising for the structure and invites her to manage the user's discomfort with it.
- **Unicorn-hunting framing**: presenting the user (or user + partner) as shopping for a third to fit a pre-built configuration. Even when the structural facts are true, framing her as a slot to fill removes her autonomy and lowers signal. Lead with her as a person; structural fit is qualification, not casting.
- **Structural disclosure as pre-emptive filter**: dropping the structure into message 1 as a defensive shield ("just so you know upfront…"). Disclosure should be matter-of-fact and embedded in the user's actual frame, not used to test her tolerance before any rapport.

The shared diagnostic: ENM disclosure is information about how the user lives, delivered with the same self-possession as any other reveal. Drafts that apologise for the structure, weaponise it, or hide it all fail the filter-not-applicant frame.

## Vulnerability anti-patterns

See `references/message-kernel.md` Vulnerability framework for the positive form. Vulnerability fails when it transfers responsibility to her, exceeds earned depth, or is used as a tactic. Reject drafts that show:

- **Trauma dump**: surfacing significant past hurt before the thread has earned it. Wounds are vulnerability Level 5; almost never landable in chat.
- **Reassurance fishing**: vulnerability whose hidden ask is "tell me I'm okay" / "tell me you still like me". The calibration question (`references/message-kernel.md`) catches these.
- **Premature soulmate energy**: "you might be different", "I feel something I haven't felt in a while", "this feels rare" before mutual revelation has happened. Intensity without rhythm reads as projection, not connection.
- **No-filter dumping**: confessional vulnerability marketed as "I just say what I feel" / "I'm just being honest". Authenticity without selectivity is a stance failure, not a strength.
- **Over-explaining standards**: long defences of why the user wants what he wants. If the standard requires a paragraph to justify, it is being argued, not held.
- **Vulnerability as conversion tactic**: deploying a reveal because charge has stalled and the user wants to re-engage her. The reveal is no longer truth — it is a lever, and she will feel it.
- **Wounded poet identity**: vulnerability as ongoing tone, not specific moment. "I feel things deeply" / "I'm a romantic in a cynical world" — identity claims, not reveals.
- **Authenticity without selectivity**: revealing the same depth to anyone who replies. Vulnerability that does not discriminate carries no signal — it is performance, not invitation.

Diagnostic: every rejected form transfers something to her — obligation, reassurance work, intensity to absorb, or identity to validate. The good form holds itself and offers her a doorway.

## Erotic-charge anti-patterns

Charge fails when desire arrives without restraint, reciprocity, or specificity. See `references/message-kernel.md` Erotic charge for the framework these violate. Reject drafts that show:

- **Crude jump**: explicit sexual language before mutual sexual frame has been established. "I want to fuck you" sent at Level 2 reciprocity skips the build and reads as pressure regardless of the user's intent.
- **Porn-brain messaging**: describing what the user wants to do to her physically before mutual context exists. Kills trust; the woman is being asked to play a role she did not opt into.
- **Over-escalation**: jumping levels on the charge ladder (`references/message-kernel.md`). She gives Level 2; the draft replies at Level 6. Most common failure mode. Drop to her level + 1 or below.
- **Interviewing desire**: clinical questions about preferences ("what turns you on?", "what are you into sexually?") before the thread has earned them. Replace with charged forms that test the same thing: "I get the sense you enjoy a little tension, but I'm not sure how much trouble you actually are."
- **Seeking permission for own desire**: "Is it okay if I say I find you attractive?" or "I hope this isn't too forward but..." The over-soften makes her manage the user's discomfort. Replace with restrained directness: "I'm finding you very easy to be curious about."
- **Fake dominance**: "Careful, I'm trouble" / "you couldn't handle me" — cringe unless held with self-awareness and humour. Replace with specific self-aware version: "I'm only trouble in highly specific, well-referenced contexts."
- **Pressure dressed as desire**: a charged line sent into a thread that has not reciprocated; broadcasting wanting at someone, not building it with them. Diagnostic: is `charge_level_attempted_max` more than `charge_level_reciprocated_max + 1` per `references/thread-state.md`? If yes, the draft is pressure.
- **Unowned vulnerability**: "I'm scared of getting hurt again" — early vulnerability that demands reassurance is not charge; it is a request she did not sign up for. Vulnerability that adds charge is small, specific, owned, lightly playful (per `references/message-kernel.md` Vulnerability as charge).

The shared diagnostic: charge is desire under restraint with reciprocity. Anti-patterns either remove the restraint (crude, porn-brain, seeking-permission) or remove the reciprocity (over-escalation, pressure, broadcasting). Fix by dropping a level and waiting for her step, not by softening the surface.
