# Decision Rubric

Use this file to choose the move: like, pass, engage, continue, clarify, escalate, revive once, deprioritise, or exit.

## Operating principle: asymmetric investment

The primary signal is **voluntary investment from the other side**, not response rate, warmth in tone, or message frequency. A reply is not investment. Investment looks like: asks back, reveals specifics, plays back, references earlier threads, suggests availability, qualifies herself against a stated standard, escalates flirtation, follows up.

Match her energy when it is good. Slightly elevate when she is increasing. **Do not compensate when she is low-effort** — that is the over-function failure and it inverts the dynamic the rubric is designed to produce.

Operational forms of this rule:
- Reply length should not materially exceed her last message length when she is `maintaining` or `decreasing`.
- Charge level should not exceed her last charge level by more than one step on the `references/message-kernel.md` Charge ladder. Concretely: `charge_level_attempted_next ≤ charge_level_reciprocated_max + 1` per `references/thread-state.md`. Skipping levels is the most common erotic-charge failure.
- A long reply to a short reply is a draft red flag; rewrite or shorten before sending.
- Carrying the energy for two consecutive cycles when she is not investing → set `deprioritized: true`.
- Carrying the charge for two consecutive cycles when she is not reciprocating it → drop the charge level back to her last reciprocated level; if that fails, treat the thread as platonic-only or apply the over-escalation deprioritise trigger below.

## Operating principle: signal-responsive routing

The skill is not a fixed funnel. The pipeline stages in `pipeline.md` define operational scaffolding — what the browser does, what gets persisted — not the conversational sequence the user must push her through. Within Stages 4–7 the conversation is **non-linear**: every incoming message is a routing signal, not a step.

**Master rule**: do not impose sequence. Read invitation. Match pace. Keep standards. Escalate cleanly.

**Operating question** for every incoming message: *what is she opening the door to right now, and do I want to walk through?*

This principle composes with asymmetric investment: that one decides *whose energy carries the thread*; this one decides *which path she is already on*. Both are checked together.

### Path classification

Recognise which conversational path is active. Paths can change mid-thread; re-classify when the signal shifts. When the path materially shapes the next move, name it in the `Read:` output line.

| Path | Signature signals | Best move | Common failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banter-first | playful tease, callback play, light topic shifts | keep light; add subtext gradually (charge ladder 1–3 across turns) | turning banter into sex talk before she has earned it |
| Depth-first | values surface early; slower pace; asks meaning-laden questions | vulnerability with edge (`references/message-kernel.md` Vulnerability framework), not therapy mode | therapist mode (`references/boundaries.md`) |
| Direct-sexual | charged language early; embodied/physical hints; explicit sexual frame | match directness; keep frame clean; pass the "fast is not careless" check below | crude jump (`references/boundaries.md` Erotic-charge anti-patterns) |
| Compatibility-first | values/structure questions early; ENM/poly framing | answer clearly and matter-of-factly; then reintroduce play | over-explaining ENM, poly manifesto (`references/boundaries.md`) |
| Low-effort validation | hot profile; weak replies; no questions back; no escalation | one playful test shot; deprioritise if no upgrade | continuing to carry energy past the test |
| Fantasy trap | high charge; vague availability; structure unclear; logistics dodged | enjoy the signal; qualify before over-investing; do not convert on charge alone | converting on charge alone (false-chemistry per `references/profile-conversation-read.md`) |

### Message classification

For every incoming message, identify the dominant category. This is orthogonal to the reciprocal-investment ladder — it classifies *what she is doing*, not how much effort she is putting in.

- **Invitation**: she is opening a door (charged hint, date-shaped suggestion, vulnerability reveal). → Step in. Match the door's register.
- **Test**: she is checking frame, confidence, or clarity. → Stay grounded; do not over-defend or over-perform. Route via Challenge response below.
- **Bid**: she is asking for attention, warmth, or play. → Reward when it carries effort; do not feed when it does not.
- **Boundary**: she is signalling a limit (slowing down, declining a topic, asking for space). → Respect immediately and explicitly; do not press or negotiate.
- **Logistics window**: she is making meeting possible (mentions schedule, location, availability). → Lead. Convert per `references/message-kernel.md` Conversion move.
- **Noise**: she is giving nothing (low-effort, generic, off-topic). → Do not feed it. Apply asymmetric investment rule above.

Most messages carry one dominant category; some carry two (an invitation that is also a test, for example). Pick the dominant one and move; surface the secondary if it materially affects the `Watch:` line.

### Signal-category gradient

Acceleration signals beyond the investment ladder. Used to decide *speed*, not *direction*.

**Green — accelerate**: asks questions back; teases the user; sexual or date-shaped hints; mirrors the user's frame; suggests availability; qualifies herself voluntarily; uses physical/embodied language; sends "you'd probably…" hypotheticals.

**Yellow — test one unknown, do not kill the vibe**: warm but vague; flirts but avoids logistics; sexual but unclear on boundaries; interested but inconsistent; chemistry without compatibility data; jokes about chaos.

**Red — exit, slow down, or hard-consent gate**: repeated low effort; dodges ENM/poly clarity; pressures or guilt-trips; pushes past the user's stated boundaries; appears intoxicated or unstable; asks the user to hide material facts; creates drama before meeting.

Yellow signals warrant one targeted test (the single missing fact, not a barrage); red signals route to the operational deprioritize triggers or to `safety.md` depending on type.

### Direct-sexual escalation: fast is not careless

When she opens a direct-sexual window, fast can be the right answer — fast means fewer steps, not no standards. Before escalating to logistics, the copilot must silently confirm:

- **Mutual frame**: she has stated alignment explicitly, not just charged ambiguity.
- **Sobriety and stability**: no signs of intoxication or volatility in the recent exchange.
- **ENM/poly disclosure complete** (when relevant): structure is on the record before logistics, not after.
- **Safer-sex expectations**: addressable plainly, not dodged.
- **Safe logistics**: location and meeting form are not coercive or risky.
- **Chaos/pressure scan**: no red signals from the gradient above.
- **User-side fit check**: does the user actually want this, or is he reacting to opportunity? If the answer is reaction, decline the door cleanly without moralising.

Failing any check does not mean refusing the path; it means slowing one step to clarify before continuing. The clean register combines desire with standards: *"I'm attracted to the energy. I also like keeping it clear, mutual, and low-chaos. What are you actually wanting?"* Both crude acceptance and over-cautious moralising fail this register.

The operational escalation triggers below still gate any in-app date proposal; the direct-sexual window does not bypass them. Hard consent gates from `safety.md` apply unchanged.

## Core dimensions

Score each low / medium / high only when evidence supports it:
- compatibility with User Model;
- attraction / chemistry potential;
- relationship or intent fit;
- emotional and communication quality;
- reciprocal investment;
- practical availability;
- real-person / real-intent signal;
- false chemistry risk.

## Move rules

### Engage
Use when there is at least one strong specific hook and no clear mismatch.

### Continue
Use when reciprocal investment is increasing or matching.

### Add charge
Use when the thread is warm but too platonic.

### Clarify
Use when attraction is present but a material compatibility field is unknown.

### Escalate
Use when warmth, reciprocity, and logistical plausibility are present. See **Operational escalation triggers** below for concrete thresholds.

### Revive once
Use when there was previous signal but the latest response is low-effort or flat.

### Deprioritise
Use when effort is consistently one-sided, ambiguity is not resolving, or the user is rationalising weak signal.

### Exit
Use when there is mismatch, disrespect, clear disinterest, or a pattern that drains attention.

## Challenge response routing

- Playful challenge -> play back with warmth.
- Compatibility probe -> answer clearly and invite their standard.
- Protective skepticism -> acknowledge, state standard, distinguish intent, invite perspective.
- Low-effort audition frame -> decline the audition frame and invite mutual curiosity.
- Boundary check -> answer plainly and respectfully.
- Disrespect -> exit or deprioritise.

## Escalation ladder

Every conversation read should identify one of:
- no escalation yet;
- add specificity;
- add play;
- add romantic subtext;
- clarify compatibility;
- invite a real-world test;
- exit.

Move up one step only when reciprocal investment supports it.

## Confidence

Use:
- **High**: multiple converging signals.
- **Medium**: enough signal for a move, some uncertainty.
- **Low**: sparse evidence; give a low-cost test.

## Operational escalation triggers

Read from `references/thread-state.md` `escalation_readiness`. Set `ready: true` only when **all** of:

- `consecutive_increasing_or_matching ≥ 3` across the most recent 4 trajectory entries (per `references/profile-conversation-read.md` reciprocal-investment ladder).
- `logistical_signal_seen === true` — at least one location/availability/schedule/timing reference from them.
- `real_revelation_user === true` AND `real_revelation_them === true` — each side has shared one personal/specific thing beyond surface banter.
- `charge_attempted === true` AND `charge_landed === true` — at least one deliberate flirtation/romantic-subtext attempt by the user that was reciprocated rather than flattened.
- `charge_level_reciprocated_max ≥ 4` — she has reciprocated at least at the personal-reveal level on the `references/message-kernel.md` Charge ladder. A thread escalating to a date proposal at Level 1–3 reciprocation is premature and tends to fail or produce a no-show.
- Confidence on the conversation read is at least MEDIUM (per `references/scoring.md`).

If any clause is false, do not escalate. Continue, add charge, or clarify per the move rules above.

## Operational deprioritize triggers

Set `deprioritized: true` and stop active drafting on this thread when **any** of:

- Two consecutive `maintaining` classifications immediately following a deliberate charge attempt.
- One `decreasing` classification with no warm reset within the next two exchanges.
- `draining` classification at any point.
- `false_chemistry_flags` raised in two or more consecutive cycles.
- Two consecutive cycles of asymmetric investment (user is supplying the energy; she is responding only).
- **Over-escalation without recovery**: `charge_level_attempted_max > charge_level_reciprocated_max + 2` AND the next exchange does not close the gap (either she does not climb or the user does not drop). Over-escalation without recovery means the user is broadcasting charge into a wall, not building it; treat as deprioritize unless she explicitly re-engages at the higher level.
- Disrespect or contempt observed (use Exit, not Deprioritize).

Deprioritized threads are not re-engaged automatically. The user may explicitly reopen.

## Dormancy and revival policy

Time is measured from `last_their_message_at` in thread state. The user has **one** revive budget per thread, ever.

| Time since their last reply | State | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–24h | active | Continue normally. |
| 24–48h after the user's opener | likely-no-signal | Do not revive. Treat as a non-match for funnel metrics. |
| 48–72h on a warm thread | quiet-but-viable | Wait. Do not revive yet. |
| 72h–7d on a warm thread | dormant | One revive permitted (`revival_state.revived_once === false`). Required content rule below. |
| 7d–30d | cold | Hard-consent re-engagement only (per `safety.md`). Treat as a new opener. |
| 30d+ | dead | Exit. No further outreach. |

**Revive content rule.** A revive must reference a specific earlier moment from the thread (a callback the other person originated, a topic they raised, a detail they revealed). Generic re-openers ("hey", "how's your week") are not allowed and burn the revive budget without producing signal. If no specific callback is available, the thread is not viable for revival; mark `dead`.

After the revive: if no response within 72h, mark `dead`. If response is `decreasing` or `maintaining`, mark `dead`. If response is `matching` or `increasing`, return to the prior stage and resume.
